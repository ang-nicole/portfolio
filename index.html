The Short Version

Some people fit roles.
I create them.

On paper, my career decisions look haphazard at best. Editorial work in a creative agency. Co-running a minimart during a pandemic. Reinventing a candy floss business?!

10-year-old me would scream.

What connects all of it isn't a job title — it's a way of operating. Noticing what's changing, moving before others do, learning whatever it takes, and refusing to stop until it works.

I don't slot neatly into a conventional career arc. That's not a gap in my profile — it's the whole point.

The Traits

Why I work the way I work

Core Trait

Curious by Default

For a decade, I wrote authoritatively about industries I'd never worked in — healthcare, law, technology, the environment — often with barely enough time to breathe before the next brief landed. What carried me through wasn't just speed, but curiosity — the need to understand things properly, not just well enough to get by. Over time, that curiosity became instinct. It drives how I approach entrepreneurship — any room I walk into that I don't yet fully understand.

Business Development New Ventures Consulting Research

Core Trait

I Spot the Trend Early

In my agency years, part of the job was finding stories and personalities worth showcasing. Not the ones already making news, but the ones who would be. The habit of seeing signal before it becomes noise is the same instinct that read COVID an opportune time for entrepreneurship. And when a supplier mentioned retirement, I didn't hear the end of a business, I saw the beginning of one. Pattern recognition at that level isn't luck. It's what happens when you've spent years training your eye on what's quietly becoming important before the crowd arrives.

Strategy Market Expansion Product Sourcing Entrepreneurship

Core Trait

Steep Curves Don't Scare Me

A tight-deadline creative agency is its own kind of boot camp. Fresh brief in the morning, polished output by evening — that was a regular Tuesday. You learn that not knowing something is never an excuse for not delivering. That conditioning carried straight into building businesses: sales, logistics, regulations, distribution networks — all unfamiliar, all figured out, all operational.

Operations Startups Industry Pivots Logistics

Core Trait

I Read People Well

For years, my job was finding extraordinary people in ordinary places. To give them a voice and to tell their stories. That skill moves beyond the interview room. It's how I built customer and supplier relationships, navigated a minimart full of daily strangers, and figured out which business partners would actually follow through. People open up when they feel genuinely seen — and I've always been pretty good at that.

Core Trait

I Know How Messaging Works

Ten years of content strategising means understanding not just language, but audience, objective, and persuasion at scale. My true superpower? Finding the right angle. Any topic, any industry, any brief — I can find the one lens that makes it interesting. That instinct continues to run deep. It's how I position a product or open a conversation with a distributor. Most people in business know what they want to say. I know how to make people actually hear it.

For what it's worth

I value experience over outcomes.

Do it with heart and pride — but nothing lasts forever, and that's fine! When it's time to close a chapter, do so with grace and gratitude. Work is something I do. It is never who I am.

I believe in having fun.

When there is room to experiment, I tend to arrive at ideas that are more original, more engaging, and ultimately more effective.

I believe in serendipity.

I don't force connections or sales. The right things find each other when the conditions are right. Which is also why, somewhat ironically, I have no social media presence of any kind.

Starting a business is a privilege.

Not because I didn't work hard or make difficult choices. But luck, upbringing, and personal circumstance made it possible for me to follow my heart. I don't take that lightly.

I am not brave by nature.

None of this happened alone. My parents' support, and friends who became business partners and walked into the unknown alongside me — they gave me the courage I didn't have by myself. I owe them that.
